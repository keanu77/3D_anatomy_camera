3D anatomy camera adapts the reference cord to its BodyParts3D vertebral canal, groups source segments for tracking, and quantizes geometry for browser delivery. Exact transformations, source structures and limitations are recorded in `public/anatomy/nerves.json` and `NOTES.nerve-assets.md`. This adaptation is approximate, is not a scan of the viewer, and has not been clinically validated. The upstream creators do not endorse this application or its registration.

Other meshes in the neural bundle come from BodyParts3D under their separately recorded terms. Retain both sets of attribution when redistributing the bundle.
